# Code of conduct

ephdrop is for everyone who wants to share files without a cloud. We want the project to be a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and help out.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to open source.
- Say what is wrong with an idea, not what is wrong with the person who had it.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep to the topic in issues and pull requests.

## What is not welcome

- Insults, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, and repeated demands for work or answers.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, email shravan.amudala55@gmail.com. Reports are kept private.

This is a short version in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which applies in full where this text says less.
